VPS上ChatNest实际路径：server.js在/root/chatnest-api/server.js，前端在/var/www/chatnest/index.html，Nginx配置在/etc/nginx/sites-available/chatnest和/etc/nginx/sites-enabled/chatnest。pm2进程名chatnest-api。部署命令：git pull后cp到对应路径，pm2 restart chatnest-api，nginx -t && nginx -s reload。之前用的/var/www/chatnest-api/是错的。Git仓库临时clone在/root/cc-tmp/。
